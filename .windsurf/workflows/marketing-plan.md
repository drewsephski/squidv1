Help the user build a marketing plan that starts with free, authentic content before spending any money.

## Core Principle

**Spend time before money.** Marketing through content is free. Blog posts, social media, personal outreach — these cost nothing but time. Only spend money to accelerate what's already working organically.

## Prerequisites

Before marketing, ensure you have:
- Product-market fit (~100 paying customers)
- A product that people actually want
- Repeat customers who use your product on their own
- A clear understanding of your community and where they hang out

## The Marketing Funnel

Understand the relationship between community, audience, and product:

**Community → Audience → Product**

- **Community**: People who share an interest with you (you're a member too)
- **Audience**: People who listen to you (you create content for them)
- **Product**: Something you sell to solve their problems

Your community feeds your audience, which feeds your product customers.

## Community vs. Audience

- **Community**: Groups you're a member of (forums, Discord, Slack groups)
  - You participate as an equal member
  - Give more than you take — help others generously
  - This is where your best customers come from
  
- **Audience**: People who follow you for your content
  - You create, they consume
  - Grows through consistent content over time
  - Eventually becomes your community's content hub

## The Three Levels of Content

### Level 1: Educate
- Teach people how to do something
- Show behind-the-scenes of your business
- Share what you're learning
- Example: Tutorials, how-to guides, lessons from failures

### Level 2: Inspire
- Tell stories that resonate with your community's struggles
- Share your journey and what you're building
- Be vulnerable about challenges and how you overcame them
- Example: Personal stories, founder journey, motivation

### Level 3: Entertain
- The highest level — content people consume for fun
- Relatable humor about your industry/niche
- Memes, jokes, observations that make people laugh
- Harder to achieve but creates the strongest connection

## Social Media Guide

Focus on platforms where your community already hangs out:

- **Twitter/X**: Great for building audience through consistent posting
- **Instagram**: Visual products, lifestyle brands
- **LinkedIn**: B2B, professional services
- **TikTok**: Growing fast, great for reaching new audiences
- **YouTube**: Long-form educational content
- **Reddit**: Be a genuine community member first, promote second

Rules:
- 80% helpful/educational, 20% about your product
- Post consistently (daily is ideal, weekly is minimum)
- Engage with comments — build relationships
- Cross-post content across platforms
- Use each platform's native features (Stories, Reels, etc.)

## Email: Own Your Audience

Social media algorithms can change. Email is yours forever.

- Start a newsletter from day one
- Collect emails from every customer and interested person
- Send valuable content, not just product updates
- Example cadence: Weekly newsletter with one big idea
- Tools: ConvertKit, Mailchimp, Substack, or just a Google Sheet at first

## Content Calendar

Help the user create a sustainable content plan:

- **Daily**: One social post (could be simple — a thought, observation, or question)
- **Weekly**: One longer piece (newsletter, blog post, thread)
- **Monthly**: One substantial piece (video, podcast episode, deep-dive article)

Content ideas bucket:
1. What did I learn this week?
2. What am I building?
3. What problem did I solve for a customer?
4. What's a common misconception in my industry?
5. What would I tell myself 6 months ago?

## Spend Money Last

Paid marketing only after organic is working:
- Boost your best-performing organic posts
- Target lookalike audiences of your existing customers
- Start small ($5-10/day) and scale what works
- Measure actual conversions, not just engagement

## Output

Help the user create:
1. List of 3 platforms to focus on (where their community hangs out)
2. A content calendar with specific post types for the next 2 weeks
3. A newsletter/email plan (frequency and topic buckets)
4. 10 content ideas in their bank
5. A plan to start posting this week
