Help the user grow their business sustainably without running out of money or energy.

## Core Principle

**Grow at the speed of profitability, not ambition.** Sustainable growth means never running out of money and never burning out. The goal is "default alive" — profitable enough to survive indefinitely without outside funding.

## Don't Spend Money You Don't Have

### The Equation
**Revenue - Costs = Profit**

Keep this positive from day one. Growth doesn't require spending money — it requires selling to more customers.

### Two Types of Costs

**Fixed Costs**: Don't scale with customers (rent, salaries, software subscriptions)
- Keep these as low as possible
- Gumroad operated with almost no fixed costs for years
- Example: Don't rent office space until you have no other choice

**Variable Costs**: Scale with customers (payment processing, hosting, materials)
- These are fine — they grow with revenue
- Aim for these to be a small percentage of each sale

### Cost-Cutting Rules

1. **No office until it hurts.** Remote work is cheaper and often more productive.

2. **Don't hire until it hurts.** "Hire when it hurts" means wait until the pain of not having someone is worse than the cost of hiring them.

3. **Use your community.** Gumroad's first designer was a creator who made Gumroad t-shirts. He later became Head of Design.

4. **Be creative with compensation.** Equity, profit-sharing, flexible hours can offset lower cash salaries.

5. **Don't spend to look successful.** Fancy offices and swag don't make a business.

## Growth Mindset

**Profitability gets you off the grid.** Once you're profitable, you have unlimited runway. Then you can grow mindfully without the pressure of running out of money.

**Grow at the speed of your customers.** Your company will grow as quickly as your customers determine. The vast majority of small businesses are never eaten by big fish — there's room for millions of profitable small businesses.

**Your failures will fade, while your successes will stick around and compound.** Don't fear small failures. Fear not learning from them.

## Fundraising (If You Must)

Most minimalist entrepreneurs don't need VC funding. But if you do raise money:

- Raise from customers, not VCs (Kickstarter, pre-sales)
- If you take investment, take it from people who believe in your mission
- Avoid terms that give up control or force unnatural growth
- Remember: Revenue is the best funding

## Avoiding Burnout

### Co-founder Relationships
- Choose carefully — you're getting married to this person
- Align on values, work style, and what "success" means
- Communicate constantly about what's working and what isn't

### Personal Sustainability
- Set boundaries — work doesn't own you
- Build systems, not dependencies on you personally
- Take vacations (even if just a few days)
- Remember why you started — to build the life you want

## Build Profitable Confidence

The ultimate goal: **Build the right business for yourself selfishly, while serving a community of others selflessly.**

Profitable confidence means:
- Knowing you can survive indefinitely
- Making decisions from a position of strength, not desperation
- Being able to say "no" to opportunities that don't fit
- Building the house you want to live in

## Output

Help the user evaluate their current situation:
1. Calculate their monthly fixed vs variable costs
2. Identify 3 costs they could eliminate or reduce
3. Create a hiring policy ("we hire when...")
4. Set a personal sustainability boundary
5. Define what "default alive" looks like for their business
